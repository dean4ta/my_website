Box Packing and Manipulation on the Agibot A2

Authors: Dean Fortier, Matthias Bothe, Rushabh Jain — for RoboService GmbH. July–September 2026.

Overview

From July to September 2026 I filled in on a RoboService team working to deliver box packing and manipulation skills on an Agibot A2 humanoid robot for Siemens. I advised on formalizing the team's model development pipeline so they could stop guessing which data works and start shipping working robot models.

The Video

The video above (shown at 2x speed) shows the working task end to end: the robot separates and picks packaging material, then performs the external pickup of the packed box. The first segment runs through separator handling; the second shows the pickup executed with the external setup.

What I Did

The team had the hardware and the ambition but was spending most of its time discovering what data would train. I brought in a structured pipeline: what to collect, how to process it, and which training techniques to apply with different models. Concretely:

  • Advised on data collection procedures for the packing and manipulation task.
  • Formalized data processing steps so collected demos turned into usable training data.
  • Recommended training techniques and model choices to match the data they had.

Results

The team went from trial-and-error data gathering to a repeatable pipeline and delivered working robot models to Siemens on schedule. The video is a demo of the final behavior running on the physical Agibot A2.
